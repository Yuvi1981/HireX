3. Client-Side Computer Vision Proctoring Suite

To prevent cheating during online skill assessments, Commit 2 embeds a browser-based AI proctoring suite using local neural network weights (client/public/models/) and @vladmandic/face-api:

Neural Model Detection Mechanism Anti-Cheat Violation Flagged
SSD MobileNet V1 Real-time bounding box face detection. Multi-person detection (flagged if >1 person in camera frame).
Tiny Face Detector Lightweight ultra-fast fallback for low-spec devices. Face absence (flagged if student leaves the desk).
Face Landmark 68 3D 68-point facial mesh tracking eyes and nose orientation. Looking away from screen / head yaw & pitch deviation.
Tab-Switch Monitor HTML5 Visibility API & window blur event listeners. Opening external tabs or searching answers in another window.
Privacy-First Architecture (DPDP Act & GDPR Compliant)
All computer vision inference happens 100% locally in the candidate's browser via WebAssembly / WebGL. Zero video streams or biometric facial images are transmitted to external servers, ensuring full compliance with the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act and global privacy laws.
